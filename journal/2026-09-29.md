## Discovery pivot (blueprint phases 1 to 4)

Decisions from Lex: all four phases; hide drops, beans, Roaster Suite and Cup Check but keep their code; community
spots go public only after admin review; store them in Supabase now.

- Phase 1, wording and tabs: `NAV_TABS` is Discover, Map & Spots, Add a Spot (`SUBMIT_TAB_ID`, new `AddSpotIcon`),
  Saved Spots; roaster portal button and drawer row removed (portal still reachable by hash). Feed segmented control
  is spot categories (`spotCategories.ts`: All, Study & Work, Quiet, Open Late); amenity shortcuts replace
  Roasteries and Beans; hero shows real study spots and approved hidden gems only (no eyebrow pill). Bean, drop and
  roastery wiring removed from `App.tsx`; old `#/bean`, `#/drop` links land on Discover, `#/roastery/<handle>` opens
  the place. Copy updated: welcome sheet, footer ("Know a hidden spot?"), profile ("Saved Spots", Add a hidden spot
  row), tour (8 steps), mood hint, index.html title and meta. "Hours not listed" replaces a false "Closed" for spots
  without hours; unknown Wi-Fi speed no longer shows "0 Mbps".
- Phase 2, directions: `DirectionsActionSheet` (Navigate in Haraya, Google Maps, Apple Maps https form, Waze) opened
  from cards, the detail sheet and Mood Finder. `useLiveNavigation` (watchPosition, high accuracy, cleared on end,
  arrival and unmount, nothing stored) and `liveNavMath` (detour 1.3, 4.8 km/h, 30 m arrival, heading from device or
  movement). Map: pulsing You dot with heading cone and accuracy ring, dashed line, top banner, progress card,
  follow-me that pauses on drag, arrival card with Aya, denied and unavailable states with external links. The old
  static mood-finder route was replaced by this. Heading uses the device heading or direction of travel; compass
  orientation was not used because iOS needs a separate permission prompt.
- Phase 3, Add a Spot: migration `20260929000000_spot_submissions.sql` (check constraints incl. Davao bounding box,
  RLS: approved public, own visible, admin all, insert only as self and pending; no client update or delete;
  trigger limits 5 per day and 10 pending; `admin_review_submission`; `handle_new_user` creates guest profiles).
  `spotService` (PKCE magic-link sign-in, load, submit, review, cache of approved spots) and `spotMapping` (validation
  mirroring the DB, row to Cafe without invented details). `AddSpotView` with sign-in card, form with
  `LocationPicker` (tap map or current location), public-place confirmation, your spots with status, admin review
  queue. Cards and sheet show Community gem or Pending review and the local tip. Privacy Notice and Terms updated.
  Startup loads community spots before pruning saves, and skips pruning if loading fails.
- Phase 4, verification: `npm test` 55/55 (6 files, 30 new tests), lint clean, `npx tsc -b` clean, `npm run build`
  946 ms (main chunk 958 kB / 278 kB gzip, up from 777 kB mainly from supabase-js; size warning still open), code map
