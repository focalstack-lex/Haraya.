## P1: Competing actions and duplicated status

### P1-1. Drop card: four actions, three of them are reminders
- **File**: `src/components/drops/BeanDropCard.tsx:111-138`
- **Evidence**: `Reserve or Inquire` (primary), `Google` (calendar), `.ics` (calendar file), `Remind me` (in-app). On a 375px card these wrap to two rows (confirmed in the Roast Drops screenshot).
- **Impact**: Three buttons answer one question ("remind me about this batch"). The row becomes the tallest part of the card and dilutes the primary action.
- **Fix**: Keep `Reserve or Inquire` as the only filled action. Fold Google, .ics and in-app reminder into one `Remind me` button that opens a small sheet or menu (In Haraya, Google Calendar, Calendar file). One row, two controls.

### P1-2. Discover hero: live status said twice, and the status chip outranks the button
- **File**: `src/components/feed/EditorialHero.tsx:48` (meta line) and the CTA row added today
- **Evidence**: Meta line "Roast drop, live now from Apo Highlands Roastery" plus cream chip "Batch is live on the shelf". The chip is solid cream, visually heavier than the translucent `View lot` button.
- **Impact**: Duplicate information; the eye lands on a non-tappable label before the real action.
- **Fix**: Move live status or countdown to a small dark status indicator in the photo's top-left (functional status, allowed by the badge rule). Meta line becomes roaster plus tasting notes. `View lot` becomes the only solid element in the bottom row, with price beside it. Same change as proposed in chat for this surface.

### P1-3. Cafe card: three text actions in a half-width card
- **File**: `src/components/feed/CafeGrid.tsx:89-122`
- **Evidence**: Action bar with `Route`, `Rate` and `Details` / `Roastery` inside a card that is about 165px wide on phones. The photo and the title already open the same detail view as `Details`.
- **Impact**: Three small targets competing in a card whose whole surface already means "open this cafe". `Route` hides its label below 400px, so phones see an icon, a star and a word.
- **Fix**: Make the card body the primary action (it already is), drop `Details` / `Roastery`, keep `Route` as an icon button, and leave `Rate` to the detail modal where it already exists (`CafeDetailModal.tsx:237-245`). The card loses one row of chrome.

## P2: Redundant copy and long text

### P2-1. Welcome sheet says the same three things twice
- **File**: `src/components/common/WelcomeModal.tsx:19-35` and `:122`
- **Evidence**: Subtitle "Cafes, micro-roasteries and single-origin beans from Davao, in one place." followed by three feature rows titled Cafe discovery, Local micro-roasteries, Single-origin bean drops. Feature bodies run 68 to 85 characters.
- **Fix**: Drop the subtitle (the feature list is the subtitle). Cut bodies to one short line each:
  - Cafe discovery: "By vibe, amenities and open now." (85 to 33 chars)
  - Local micro-roasteries: "Who roasts nearby, and what is on the shelf." (68 to 44)
  - Single-origin bean drops: "Reserve small batches from the roaster." (74 to 39)
  - About 55% fewer words on the sheet.

### P2-2. Welcome sheet: two controls do the same thing
- **File**: `src/components/common/WelcomeModal.tsx:101-110` and `:158-163`
- **Evidence**: The top-right X ("Skip and explore") and the bottom link "Continue as guest" both call `onClose`.
- **Fix**: Keep one. On phones the X is the expected sheet dismiss, so remove "Continue as guest" and let "Get started" plus "Log in" be the only choices. Saves a 44px row.

### P2-3. Trail cards print the full description
- **File**: `src/components/map/DavaoCoffeeMap.tsx:339`
- **Evidence**: `trail.description` rendered without a clamp; the longest is 182 characters (`src/data/mockTrails.ts:8`), directly above a full stop list that already names every cafe.
- **Fix**: `line-clamp-2` on the description. The stop list carries the detail.

### P2-4. Drop card metadata stacks two time readouts
- **File**: `src/components/drops/BeanDropCard.tsx:98-107`
- **Evidence**: Countdown `00:20:59:59` and absolute time `Sep 29, 3:34 PM` side by side, then a separate price line.
- **Fix**: Show the countdown only when under 24 hours, otherwise the date only. Merge price into the same row: `₱680 / bag, 40 in batch`.
