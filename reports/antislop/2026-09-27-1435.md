## Per-Lens Findings

### Lens 1: Visual and Typography Slop
**Score: 0.5 / 10 (Slop-Free / Excellent)**

- **Shape Contrast & Corner Radii:** Complies with shape hierarchy standards. Hero banner uses `rounded-2xl sm:rounded-3xl` ([EditorialHero.tsx:50](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Haraya/src/components/feed/EditorialHero.tsx#L50)), product cards use `rounded-2xl sm:rounded-3xl` ([CafeGrid.tsx:28](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Haraya/src/components/feed/CafeGrid.tsx#L28)), modal form inputs use `rounded-xl` ([FormControls.tsx:80](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Haraya/src/components/common/FormControls.tsx#L80)), and interactive buttons use `rounded-full`.
- **Color Scarcity (80/15/5 Rule):** Warm Cream linen canvas (`#FBF4E4` / `#FFF9E9`) and deep obsidian slate (`#1A2225`) comprise over 92% of the visual surface. Terracotta Roast accent (`#C86428`) is kept strictly scarce and applied only to focal points (micro-lot badges, live countdowns, active tab indicators).
- **Product Imagery Systems:** Fixed aspect ratios enforced across all media cards: `aspect-[4/3]` on cafes and beans, `aspect-[4/5]` on community cup check cards, and `aspect-[16/10]` on detail modal snap galleries with `object-cover`.
- **Anti-Emoji Directive:** 0 emojis found in UI, badges, pills, toasts, or markdown.
- **Anti-Em-Dash Directive:** 0 em-dashes (`—`) or en-dashes (`–`) found across all copy.
- **Development & Framework Artifacts:** Clean viewport; zero floating dev widgets or builder overlay icons.

### Lens 2: Structural and UX Slop
**Score: 0.8 / 10 (Slop-Free / High Utility)**

- **Commercial Utility vs Component Showcase:** The interface is built for specific tasks: discovering local Davao specialty coffee shops by neighborhood, viewing verified Wi-Fi speeds and opening hours, reserving whole bean lots, and logging cup tasting notes.
- **Badge Discipline:** Zero generic "100% Genuine / Official Quality" decorative pills. Badges are strictly functional metadata: live status (`● Open Now` / `Closed`), price tier (`₱₱`), and limited micro-lot tags.
- **Information Architecture:** Customer discovery tabs (Discover, Bean Drops, Coffee Map, Cup Check, Saved) are clearly separated from administrative verification and roaster management portals ([NavigationHeader.tsx:11-29](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Haraya/src/components/layout/NavigationHeader.tsx#L11-L29)).
- **Minor Observation [P3]:** The Admin Control Room toggle is visible in the portal role switcher during guest browsing.  
  *Proposed Fix:* Keep guest view focused strictly on "Roaster Sign In" while isolating the admin route to authenticated administrators.
